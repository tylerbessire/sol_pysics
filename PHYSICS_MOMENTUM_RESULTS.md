# 🚀 PHYSICS MOMENTUM DECAY STRATEGY - PROFITABLE!

## Strategy Summary

**Core Physics Principle:**
- Momentum = mass × velocity (volume × price_velocity)
- Like a rocket or ball: launches with high momentum → peaks → fuel runs out → momentum decays → gravity takes over → falls back down

**Entry Rules:**
1. Track $1-2 USD climbs
2. Monitor momentum decay (drops to 50-60% of peak)
3. Wait for red candle confirmation (gravity takes over)
4. Enter SHORT

**Exit Rules:**
- TP: 0.4% drop (quick scalp, achievable in 2 candles)
- SL: 0.5% above entry (tight risk management)
- **TIMEOUT: 2 candles maximum hold** (if gravity doesn't work fast, exit)
- Leverage: 50x
